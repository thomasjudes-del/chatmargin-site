Follow-up + Search

Stop forgetting which ChatGPT conversations still need work.

ChatMargin adds follow-up status, priorities, a Dashboard and Search around your chats so unfinished work is easier to resume.

How can I organize unfinished ChatGPT conversations?ChatMargin lets you mark a conversation for follow-up, assign a priority and return to it from a dedicated Dashboard. Search helps you find known conversations and related ChatMargin material later.

The problem is not always finding a chat

ChatGPT can contain many conversations that are technically easy to reopen but operationally hard to manage. The bigger problem is often remembering which ones matter, which are unfinished and what you were supposed to do next.

ChatMargin treats that as a workflow problem rather than a folder problem.

Follow-up and priorities

  • Mark a conversation when there is still something to do.
  • Assign a priority so important unfinished chats stand out.
  • Use the Dashboard as a return point for work that is still open.
  • Keep next steps in the note attached to the conversation instead of relying on memory.

Search across the ChatMargin layer

The Search tab is designed to help you retrieve material around your ChatGPT work. It can search known conversation metadata together with ChatMargin notes, note sub-pages and prompts.

Important limitation

ChatMargin does not claim to be a complete full-text index of every ChatGPT transcript. Search is focused on the ChatMargin working layer and the conversation metadata ChatMargin knows about.

A practical workflow for long-running work

During the conversation

Keep the important context and next steps in Notes. If the work is unfinished, mark the conversation for follow-up and set a priority.

Between sessions

Use the Dashboard to see open work instead of relying on browser history or memory.

When you cannot remember where something lives

Use Search to look across the material ChatMargin has stored around your conversations.

Turn unfinished chats into a manageable queue.
