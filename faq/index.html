Frequently asked questions

Questions people ask about working with ChatGPT.

Direct answers about what ChatMargin does, what it does not do, and how it fits around ChatGPT.

Is there a way to take notes next to ChatGPT conversations?

Yes. ChatMargin adds persistent notes beside individual ChatGPT conversations. Notes support rich formatting, lists, checklists, links, sub-pages and a larger Workspace view.

How can I keep notes attached to a specific ChatGPT chat?

ChatMargin associates its note workspace with the current ChatGPT conversation, so you can return to that conversation and continue with the relevant notes beside it.

Is there a Chrome extension for taking notes inside ChatGPT?

ChatMargin is a Chrome and Chromium extension built specifically to add notes and other working tools beside ChatGPT.

Can I save and reuse prompts in ChatGPT?

Yes. ChatMargin includes a Prompts area where reusable prompts can be saved, reordered and inserted into the ChatGPT composer.

How do I keep track of unfinished ChatGPT conversations?

You can mark a conversation for follow-up in ChatMargin, add priority when useful and return to unfinished work from the Dashboard.

Can I search my ChatGPT notes and prompts?

ChatMargin Search covers known conversation metadata and ChatMargin content such as notes, note sub-pages and prompts.

Is there a Notion-like workspace for ChatGPT?

ChatMargin includes a larger Workspace for conversation-linked notes. It is intentionally narrower than a general tool like Notion: the goal is to give your ChatGPT side notes more space without disconnecting them from the chat.

Does ChatMargin store my notes on a server?

The current product is local-first. ChatMargin notes, prompts, follow-up data and related metadata are stored in Chrome extension storage, and no ChatMargin account is required.

Does ChatMargin back up my full ChatGPT conversations?

No. ChatMargin is not positioned as a full transcript backup or replacement for ChatGPT conversation history. It stores the working layer you create around conversations.

Is ChatMargin affiliated with OpenAI?

No. ChatMargin is an independent product and is not affiliated with OpenAI or ChatGPT.
