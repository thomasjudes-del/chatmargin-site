What ChatMargin adds

ChatGPT is the conversation. ChatMargin is the layer around it.

Built for people who use ChatGPT across long, complex or unfinished work and need a lightweight system for everything that should stay beside the chat.

Notes

Notes attached to each conversation

Capture context, decisions and next steps with rich formatting, lists, checklists, links and separate sub-pages.

Prompts

Save, reorder and reuse prompts

Keep prompts close to the work and insert them directly into the ChatGPT composer when needed.

Dashboard

Remember what still needs attention

Mark conversations for follow-up, assign priority and return to unfinished work from a dedicated dashboard.

Search

Find the material around your chats

Search across known conversation metadata, ChatMargin notes, sub-pages and prompts.

Workspace

Move from sidebar to a full writing surface

Open the same note in a larger Workspace view while keeping it tied to the same conversation.

Continuity

Use Context Checkpoint in long chats

Context Checkpoint is available from each conversation as a dedicated continuity tool for long or complex work.

Local-first

Your ChatMargin data stays in your browser.

ChatMargin is designed to work without a ChatMargin account or a ChatMargin backend storing your notes.

  • Local storage: ChatMargin notes, prompts, follow-up data and related metadata use Chrome extension storage.
  • No ChatMargin account: the current product does not require you to create an account.
  • Not a transcript archive: ChatMargin is not positioned as a replacement for ChatGPT conversation history or as a full-chat backup tool.
  • Independent product: ChatMargin is not affiliated with OpenAI or ChatGPT.
  • Read the full Privacy Policy.
