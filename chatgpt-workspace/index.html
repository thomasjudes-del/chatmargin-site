ChatMargin / ChatGPT workspace
Sidebar + Workspace

Add a working notebook beside ChatGPT.

ChatMargin lets you capture and structure work beside a ChatGPT conversation, then open the same note in a larger Workspace when the task needs more room.

Is there a Notion-like workspace for ChatGPT?ChatMargin provides a lightweight local workspace around ChatGPT: conversation-linked notes, rich formatting, sub-pages, prompts, follow-ups, search and a larger Workspace view. It is not intended to replace a full knowledge-management platform.

Why a sidebar alone is not enough

A sidebar is ideal for capture because it stays visible next to the conversation. But serious work can quickly outgrow a narrow panel. ChatMargin combines both modes: quick work beside ChatGPT and a larger Workspace for the same note.

What the Workspace model gives you

  • Conversation-linked notes that stay close to the relevant chat.
  • Rich formatting for structured working notes.
  • Sub-pages for splitting complex material into distinct sections.
  • A larger Workspace surface when you need more room than the sidebar provides.
  • Access to the broader ChatMargin system: Prompts, Dashboard and Search.

When this is useful

Research and synthesis

Keep sources to check, hypotheses, conclusions and open questions outside the conversation stream while still linking them to the chat where the analysis happened.

Consulting and project work

Separate meeting notes, decisions, deliverable structure and next actions into sub-pages instead of scattering them across several tools.

Writing and product thinking

Use the conversation for iteration and the Workspace for structure, outlines, decisions and reusable context.

Local-first by design

The current product uses Chrome extension storage and does not require a ChatMargin account. This makes ChatMargin deliberately lighter than a cloud workspace product.

How this differs from a general notes app

A general notes app starts from documents and pages. ChatMargin starts from the ChatGPT conversation and adds the surrounding working layer. Its purpose is not to become another destination you must manage, but to reduce the context switching around ChatGPT.
