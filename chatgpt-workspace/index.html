Guide

How to add a real working notebook beside ChatGPT.

ChatMargin starts as a lightweight sidebar, but when your note becomes substantial you can open the same content in a larger Workspace without disconnecting it from the conversation.

Short answerUse ChatMargin beside ChatGPT for quick notes and prompts, then open Workspace when you need a larger writing surface. The content remains part of the same ChatMargin context around that conversation.

Why a workspace matters

A sidebar is excellent for quick capture but weak for longer thinking. Once a note contains structure, several sub-pages, checklists or a developing plan, a narrow panel becomes restrictive. Workspace gives that same material more room.

From quick capture to deeper work

  • Capture ideas directly beside the current ChatGPT conversation.
  • Structure larger work with note sub-pages.
  • Open the note in Workspace for a wider editing surface.
  • Return to the sidebar without creating a separate disconnected document.
Not a general Notion replacement

ChatMargin's Workspace is intentionally centered on the ChatGPT workflow. Its purpose is to give conversation-linked notes enough room, not to become a general-purpose company wiki or database.

When Workspace is useful

Typical examples include a research plan that grows during a chat, a client deliverable outline, a product backlog, a long list of decisions and assumptions, or a writing project that needs both ChatGPT and structured side notes.
